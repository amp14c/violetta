PATH ridership, 2012–2026

What 15 years of faregate data say about how New Jersey commutes to Manhattan

AP

The question

PATH links Newark, Harrison, Jersey City and Hoboken with Manhattan. It has 13 stations and carried about 280,000 riders a weekday before 2020.

What happened to PATH ridership?

  • How deep was the 2020 collapse?
  • How far has it recovered?
  • Has the shape of the commute changed?

The catch: no usable data

The Port Authority publishes ridership only as PDFs, with no API and no CSV. The free datasets either stopped in 2016 (NY Open Data) or have no station or hourly detail (National Transit Database).

What I built

1 · Extract

Downloaded 27 PDF reports (more than 2,000 pages) and parsed every table with Python (pdfplumber).

2 · Validate

Every month’s stations add up exactly to the printed totals. The results match NY Open Data within 0.33%, and their movements track the federal NTD.

3 · Publish

Clean CSVs, an interactive dashboard and these slides, all from one shared chart module so the numbers always match.

Reading the numbers

A trip = one person entering through a faregate, counted at the station where the trip starts. Exits (where trips end) appear only in the hourly data.

Ridership fell 67% in 2020; in 2025 it was 74% of 2019

82M trips in 2019 → 27M in 2020. Lowest month April 2020. Steady climb since.

The commute is smaller and less concentrated

Rush hours carry a smaller share of the day: 48.7% → 44.2%

Share of weekday entries in 7–9 am and 5–7 pm. A flatter day suggests hybrid work and more off-peak travel.

New Jersey stations start the day; New York stations end it

Entries as a share of each station’s day. Every NJ station peaks at 7–8 am; every NY station peaks at 5 pm, the trip home.

Limitations

  • Entries ≠ people. A daily commuter makes about 40 trips a month, and cross-honored and freewheeled entries are excluded.
  • Closures look like near-zero values (e.g. Hoboken, Feb 2025), not missing data.
  • Hourly detail starts in 2017, plus two one-month snapshots (2014, 2016).
  • Fare-system change (TAPP, 2023–25) altered the counting equipment.
